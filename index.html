THE STORY OF FORBIDDEN ISLAND

Forbidden Island

Forbidden Island was the secluded retreat of the Archeans, an ancient empire said to command fire, wind, water and earth through four sacred treasures. To protect them, the Archeans designed the island to sink if anyone tried to claim them. Now the island has been rediscovered. Can your team recover the treasures and escape?

FIELD GUIDE · 01

Your mission

1. Take up to three actions

Move, shore up, give a treasure card to a teammate, or capture a treasure with four matching cards at its site. Role powers help your crew.

2. Draw treasure, then flood cards

Draw two Treasure cards. Then reveal Flood cards equal to the water level. A flooded tile that floods again sinks.

3. Escape together

Capture all four treasures, gather every explorer at Fools’ Landing, then use a Helicopter Lift card.
