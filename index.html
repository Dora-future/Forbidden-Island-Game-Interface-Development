THE STORY OF FORBIDDEN ISLAND

Forbidden Island

Forbidden Island was the secluded retreat of the Archeans, an ancient empire said to command fire, wind, water and earth through four sacred treasures. To protect them, the Archeans designed the island to sink if anyone tried to claim them. Now the island has been rediscovered. Can your team recover the treasures and escape?

FIELD GUIDE · 01

How to play

Work together

Move around the island, shore up flooded tiles, and help each other collect the four treasures.

Take your turn

Use up to three actions, draw two treasure cards, then resolve the flood cards shown by the water level.

Escape together

Collect all four treasures, gather every explorer at Fool’s Landing and use a Helicopter Lift.
